Read this as

  • Same trade in both: the live bot and the backtest both took the trade from the same signal. Live and backtest agreeing on most trades means the backtest is a fair picture of what the bot does.
  • Live only: the bot traded but the backtest didn't (for instance a slot or margin limit hit differently, or the settings changed). A trade opened after the newest stored candle can't have a backtest twin yet, so a recent one here is normal.
  • Backtest only: the backtest traded but the bot missed it (bot was paused or scanning late, funding was too high at that moment, price had moved outside the stop/target range).
  • Entry vs backtest positive means live paid more than the backtest assumed (the backtest fills at the next candle's open, live fills when it notices).
