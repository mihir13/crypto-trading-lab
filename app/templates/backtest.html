What to keep in mind

Built in to keep results honest

  • A signal on a candle's close is acted on at the next candle's open, never the same one (tests prove no lookahead).
  • Every fill pays your slippage setting against you, plus the taker fee.
  • If one candle touches both stop and target, the stop is assumed to hit first. A stop that gaps fills at the worse price.
  • Real funding rates while a trade is open (Bitget's own for the last ~90 days, Binance's as a proxy before that).
  • Isolated-margin liquidation is modelled.
  • Live bot and backtest run the same strategy code and the same cost model.

Not modelled: real results can be worse

  • Order-book depth and market impact (fine for small size, wrong for large).
  • Partial fills, exchange downtime, API latency, stops skipped in fast markets.
  • The order of highs and lows inside a candle (candles only give open/high/low/close).
  • Survivorship: SOL, AVAX and LINK are coins that still exist today. Ones that collapsed aren't in the sample.
  • One market regime: 2021 to 2026 was mostly falling for these coins. A different regime can behave differently.
  • One out-of-sample split is one roll of the dice; treat it as evidence, not proof.
